## 1. What the search data says

### The big shift: class beats brand
- **"GLP-1" has overtaken "Ozempic" as the dominant search term** — 3M+ monthly US queries as of 2025, while "Ozempic" declined from its 6M peak. Searchers now research the *drug class* and compare providers, instead of asking for one brand. Our campaign's GLP-1-first language is exactly where demand moved — keep it.
- High-intent modifiers dominate the buying stage: **"near me" / "cerca de mí," "cost," "precio," "cuánto cuesta," "$ al mes."**

### The Hispanic demand signal
- **13% of Hispanic adults have taken a GLP-1** (vs 10% of White adults) and **22% of non-users say they're interested** — demand is above-average in our target demo.
- **Affordability is the #1 friction: ~half of users say GLP-1s are hard to afford, and 33% of Hispanic adults report trouble paying for prescriptions.** Price-transparent Spanish copy ("$199 al mes, todo incluido, sin cargos ocultos") is not just an offer — it is *the* answer to the most-searched concern.
- Competitor clinics in Florida and beyond are already building Spanish local-SEO pages for "inyecciones de semaglutida para bajar de peso + [city]" — the pattern proves those queries carry volume, but almost no one owns them in Miami-Dade yet.

## 2. The vocabulary decision (this is the copy answer)

**Lead with "bajar de peso" everywhere.** It's the phrase South Florida Latinos search and say. Rules:

1. **"Bajar de peso"** = headlines, CTAs, keywords. (Current ads already use "Baja de peso" — correct; extend it to subheads.)
2. **"Pérdida de peso"** = clinical/supporting lines only ("programa médico de pérdida de peso") — it reads professional but nobody *searches* it.
3. **Avoid "adelgazar"** as primary — Spain-leaning register, under-searched by US Latinos.
4. **"Rebajar"** — the Cuban/Caribbean colloquial ("quiero rebajar") — is a Hialeah-audience gem. Use in social captions, radio scripts, and testimonial voice ("Por fin pude rebajar esas libras"), not in formal medical copy.
5. Keep **"GLP-1"** as the class term in all creative; name Ozempic®/Wegovy® **nowhere** in paid media.
6. Always pair price with completeness: **"$199 al mes · Todo incluido · Sin cargos ocultos"** — this answers the affordability anxiety that KFF shows is the #1 blocker.
