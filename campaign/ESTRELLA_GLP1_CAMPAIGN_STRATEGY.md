# Estrella Medical Centers — GLP-1 Weight Loss Campaign
### "Tu Nueva Estrella" · South Florida Hispanic Market · August 2026

**Offer:** Programa médico de pérdida de peso con GLP-1 — ~~$299/mes~~ **$199/mes por tiempo limitado**
**Audience:** Hispanic/Latino adults 30–60, South Florida (Miami-Dade, Broward, Hialeah, Kendall, Doral, West Palm)
**Languages:** Spanish-first, with bilingual variants for 2nd-gen audiences

## 1. Market Analysis — What's Working in GLP-1 Advertising Right Now

### The category
- GLP-1 patients are **high-intent researchers**: they compare providers, read reviews, and decide fast — often researching at night on mobile. Ads must answer "why you, how much, is it safe" within the first frame.
- The four creative angles that consistently convert in GLP-1 telehealth are **problem-led, authority (doctor-led), social proof, and price/comparison**.
- **Cost is the #1 churn driver** — patients quit when it feels financially unsustainable. A transparent flat monthly price ($199) is itself a conversion asset; lead with it.
- **Access is commoditized; trust is not.** National telehealth brands (Hims/Hers, Ro, Noom) own the low-price/online space. A brick-and-mortar *medical center* wins on the opposite axis: real doctors, in-person care, ongoing supervision, community presence. Estrella should never try to out-cheap telehealth — it should out-*trust* them.

### The compliance reality (this matters — the FDA is actively enforcing)
- The FDA has issued **thousands of warning letters since Sept 2025** (30 in March 2026 alone) to companies making misleading claims about compounded GLP-1s. Avoid: naming Ozempic®/Wegovy® in ads, "same active ingredient" claims, guaranteed results, and specific-pounds promises.
- **Meta bans before/after body imagery**, negative self-perception triggers (tape measures, pinching fat, sad "before" states), and idealized-results claims in health ads. Prescription products additionally require Meta's written permission / LegitScript pathway — the workaround for top-of-funnel is advertising the *clinic program and consultation*, not the drug.
- Every ad should carry: *"Se requiere evaluación y receta médica. Los resultados varían. Supervisado por médicos."*

### The Hispanic market opportunity
- **56% of Hispanics respond better to Spanish-language ads** than English for services like healthcare — and most competitors in this category are running English-only creative. That's the gap.
- Hispanic consumers are **mobile-first and video-heavy** (81% digital-video engagement, highest of any group). Top platforms: **Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram** — note WhatsApp, which almost no medical competitor uses as a CTA.
- **Doctors are the single most trusted messenger** in Hispanic healthcare marketing. Creative showing a physician (ideally one who "habla tu idioma") outperforms lifestyle-only creative.
- Cultural resonance beats translation. Family motivation ("hazlo por ellos"), food culture kept *positive* (never "give up your abuela's cooking" — instead "keep enjoying it"), music/dance, and faith-adjacent perseverance ("sí se puede") are the levers.

The star ("estrella") is a gift of a brand device — use it as the visual motif everywhere.

## 3. The Campaign: "Tu Nueva Estrella" ✦

Big idea: *You are the star of this transformation — and your medical team is behind you.* Works as a double meaning (the clinic's name + the patient's own moment) and yields a repeatable motif: the gold star.

### Funnel mapping
- **Awareness:** 1, 6, 9, 10 (emotion, culture, brand)
- **Consideration:** 2, 5, 7 (education, authority, proof)
- **Conversion:** 3, 8 (offer, low-friction WhatsApp CTA)

### Standing compliance rules for all creative
1. Advertise the **program and consultation**, never the drug by brand name. Say "medicamentos GLP-1 recetados por médicos."
2. No before/after, no tape measures, no negative body framing, no specific weight-loss promises.
3. Always include: *"Se requiere evaluación médica. Los resultados varían."*
4. Overlay the real Estrella logo in post-production (top or bottom of each canvas has reserved clean space) — never let AI redraw the logo.
5. Add real phone/address/URL in post. Creatives ship CTA-ready with space reserved.
