# Estrella Medical Centers — Services Campaign ("Cuidamos de Ti")
### Moodboard 2 analysis + 10 service-line creatives · August 2026

## Moodboard 2 critique

**Why this board is the right direction:** unlike the green GLP-1 mood board, this design system (Brazilian-style clinic social templates) is *already* Estrella's brand world — blue-dominant, white space, medical trust cues. Adapting it means the feed looks unified with the logo instead of fighting it. We keep our one differentiator: the **gold star accent** (no competitor clinic design in this style uses a warm accent — instant recognition).

| Template | Design lesson worth stealing | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| "Novembro Azul" awareness | Giant stacked type interlocking with a photo cutout = premium editorial feel; awareness-month hooks give a *reason* to post | Portuguese layout only — rebuild, don't translate mechanically |
| Medinfin doctor + shapes | Geometric brand shapes + pill-shaped CTA button ("Agende sua consulta") reads modern and clickable | Generic stock doctor; ours must read Hispanic and warm |
| Ortho Clinic "dia do" | Date-badge + broken/hyphenated stacked type is distinctive | Niche day-of posts are organic content, not paid ads |
| Raio X price card | Service + transparent price + WhatsApp number = pure conversion format | Don't invent prices we haven't verified; use it for the $199 GLP-1 offer |
| Diabetes question post | Question-headline ("¿Qué debo saber…?") matches how people actually search | Needs a CTA — the original has none beyond "read caption" |
| Vaccine 30min/3h/3d | Numbered-steps timeline is the clearest educational format on the board | Keep to 3 steps max |
| Fisioterapia dark | Dark navy dramatic variant adds range so the feed isn't monotone | Skeleton imagery can feel cold — use sparingly |
| Health-accounting portrait | Curved gradient + portrait + stat column = perfect trust/about format | — |

**System rules extracted for all 10 creatives:** blue scale (#0E4C92→#4FA3E3) + white + gold star accent · huge condensed stacked type interlocking with photo cutouts · geometric shapes/curves · pill CTA buttons · every ad closes with **EstrellaWellness.com · 305.217.7553** · Spanish-first · real logo composited from `generations/refs/estrella_logo.png` (never AI-redrawn).
