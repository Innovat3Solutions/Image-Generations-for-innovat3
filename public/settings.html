Assignment — who owns new prospects?

Ownership drives everything personal: the Mine filter, each rep's queues, and their calendar feed. Pick how prospects get an owner. Either way, an owner's first touch still claims an unowned prospect.

Automation — what happens when a deal closes

Won (status → Customer): the welcome + onboarding-docs emails queue automatically and the rep gets to-dos (docs, assets, kickoff, route the build to the team). Lost (status → Not interested): the reactivation funnel fires — a no-hard-feelings text and email with the free Facebook group invite, then a 90-day resurface.

Client accounts

Who are we prospecting for? Each client account holds its target niches and territory. Runs made for an account tag every prospect to it — the dashboard's account switcher shows one client's book at a time, and Export CSV delivers exactly that list for their CRM.
