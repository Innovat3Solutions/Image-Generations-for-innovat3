### Typography
- **Headlines:** Space Grotesk (bold, geometric, modern)
- **Body/UI:** Inter

### Logo
Red/blue chrome shield with winged caduceus; quadrants for **Hogar · Auto · Comercial · Salud**; wordmark "JAVI'S Insurance Services"; tagline **"Protegiendo lo que más importa."**

### Voice
- Bilingual by default — Spanish leads, English supports (Miami Spanglish is a feature, not a bug).
- Family-first, warm, local: cafecito, ventanitas, dominó, hurricanes, Miami traffic.
- Always reinforce: **GRATIS** (free help), **en tu idioma**, licensed agents, multiple carriers.

### Signature visual motif — "El Escudo" (The Shield)
Every creative re-draws the logo's shield as living light in the scene: cafecito steam becomes a shield, neon traffic trails become a shield, a storm dome becomes a shield, domino tiles form a shield. This is the ownable device that makes the series feel like one campaign while every image stays unique — one step more creative than the reference boards' literal glowing shields.

## 2. Keyword & market research (basis for the 10 concepts)

National insurance queries run ~42M searches/month; auto searches grew ~38% YoY and home ~45% (post-hurricane pricing pressure), business insurance +89%. For the South Florida Hispanic market, the highest-intent themes are:

| # | Search theme (EN/ES) | Why it matters in Miami |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "obamacare miami", "seguro médico barato", "$0 health insurance" | Miami-Dade is the #1 ACA enrollment county in the U.S.; Hispanic families over-index on marketplace plans |
| 2 | "Medicare en español", "Medicare Advantage Miami" | Huge 65+ Cuban/Colombian/Venezuelan population; AEP Oct 15–Dec 7 |
| 3 | "cheap car insurance Miami", "aseguranza de carro barata" | FL has among the highest auto premiums in the nation; "aseguranza" is the high-volume US-Spanish colloquial term |
| 4 | "home insurance Florida", "hurricane insurance" | FL homeowners crisis + hurricane season (Jun 1–Nov 30) drives spiking seasonal volume |
| 5 | "open enrollment 2026 deadline" | Deadline-driven spikes Nov 1 – Jan 15 |
| 6 | "dental insurance florida", "seguro dental" | Low-premium add-on, high family appeal |
| 7 | "business insurance Miami", "seguro comercial" | Fastest-growing category (+89%); Miami small-business density |
| 8 | "cotizar seguro por whatsapp" | WhatsApp is the default channel for Hispanic consumers; Javi's has a WhatsApp line |
| 9 | "Medicaid Florida application help", "ayuda con Medicaid" | Service Javi's offers free; constant volume |
| 10 | Brand terms: "insurance agent near me en español" | Trust/brand anthem to retarget all of the above |
