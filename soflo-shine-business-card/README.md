## Print specs

- Trim **3.5 × 2 in** (US standard). Artboards are **1050 × 600 px**, i.e. 300 dpi at trim size.
- Send with **0.125 in bleed** (3.75 × 2.25 in). The black field and the blue left bar run to the
  edge, so they can be pulled out. Nothing important sits within 0.125 in of the trim.
- Suggested stock: matte or soft-touch black, blue in spot gloss or foil.

## Type

Anton (headline), Saira 900 Italic (wordmark), Barlow Condensed (everything else) — all Google Fonts.

## Placeholders to replace before printing

- `[@HANDLE]` on the back — the real Instagram handle.
- The QR encodes `https://soflodetail.com`. It scans, but swap it for the booking URL you want.
- The car mark is a rebuild, not the original logo file. Drop in the real artwork before printing.
