    Good to know before you order

    The straight answers, from the bakers themselves.

    How far ahead do I need to order?
    Pre-orders need 9 days of lead time since everything is baked fresh to your date. Need something big like catering? Text (939) 639-7268 and we'll figure it out.
    How do payments work?
    Full payment locks in your order, through Venmo, Cash App or Zelle, within a couple hours of placing it. Pre-orders can't take cash, but the self-serve Snack Station happily does.
    How does pickup work?
    On your date we text you a cooler slot number and passcode at 679 Alexander Ave. Pickup is free. Please pick up the same day, or your snacks find a new home the next day.
    Do you deliver?
    Yes. $10 within Deltona, $15 to surrounding cities, as late as 11 PM. Shipping outside the area is temporarily paused, follow the socials for when it returns.
    What about allergens?
    The kitchen uses eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, dairy and wheat. Every item is labeled, so please double check before ordering if you have an allergy.
    Can I change my order after placing it?
    Once an order is placed it can't be edited, since ingredients get planned per batch. Message us right away if something is wrong and we'll do our best.
    Do you sell at farmers markets?
    When we do, we announce every appearance on the Facebook page and Instagram first. Keep an eye on the socials.
