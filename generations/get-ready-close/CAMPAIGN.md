# Get Ready Close (GRC Lending) | Campaña "Listo Para Cerrar"

Full ad and marketing campaign for GRC Lending, a Miami based mortgage lender.
Target audience: Hispanic homebuyers and homeowners across South Florida.
Language: Spanish first with natural Miami Spanglish. No em dashes anywhere in copy.

## Brand facts pulled from the site

- Name: GRC Lending / Get Ready Close
- Tagline: "Your South Florida Mortgage Experts"
- Positioning: premier full service mortgage company in Miami. Luxury homes, investment properties, first time buyers, 25+ years of combined expertise, education first, transparent closings.
- Products: Conventional, FHA (low down payment), VA (zero down, no PMI), Non-QM / DSCR and bank statement loans for investors.
- Contact: grclending.com | (305) 201-0472 | info@grclending.com
- Compliance: NMLS# 2117171. Equal Housing Lender.
- Logo: black GRC monogram with red, green and cyan racing stripes plus "GET READY CLOSE" wordmark. The stripes read as speed, which powers the campaign's core promise: fast, clean closings.
- Web fonts: Manrope (sans) and Playfair Display (serif) for brand-consistent typography.

## Campaign platform

**Big idea: "Listo Para Cerrar."** Every concept lands on the same promise, you get ready, we close.
The racing stripes from the logo become the campaign's visual signature: three diagonal
color bars (rojo, verde, cian) appear as an accent device in every execution.

Umbrella hashtags: #ListoParaCerrar #GetReadyClose #MiamiEsTuCasa

## The 10 concepts

### 01. Cafecito y Llaves
Miami ventanita culture. A colada cup next to a set of house keys on the counter.
- Headline: "Tu cafecito. Tus llaves. Tu casa."
- Sub: "Empieza tu pre-aprobación en lo que dura un cafecito."
- CTA: Hablemos hoy. (305) 201-0472
- Placement: IG/FB feed 4:5, Little Havana OOH.

### 02. La Propuesta

- Headline: "¿Le dices que sí?"
- Sub: "Dile sí a tu casa propia. Del papeleo nos encargamos nosotros."
- CTA: Comienza en grclending.com
- Placement: IG feed 4:5, Valentine's and Mother's Day pushes.

### 03. El Gran Estreno
