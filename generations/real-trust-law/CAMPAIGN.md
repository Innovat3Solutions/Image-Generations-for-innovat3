# Real Trust Law — "Protect What You've Built" Campaign
**Client:** Natalie Ruiz, Esq. — Real Trust Law (South Florida)
**Practice anchors:** Estate Planning · Wills & Trusts · Probate/Legacy Planning · Residential & Commercial Real Estate
**Campaign platform:** *Protect what you've built. Preserve what comes next.*

This campaign was also run as a **10-model identity-consistency bake-off**: each of the ten concepts was generated on a *different* image-generation model (10 models, 9 vendors), all fed the same reference photographs of Natalie, so the client can judge which model holds her likeness best. See `MODEL-BAKEOFF.md` for the scorecard.

> **Direction update (mid-project, per client):** the deliverables are clean photographic **website scenes with no baked-in typography** — files named `*_scene_*.jpg`. The concept sheets below still document the full campaign thinking (headlines, CTAs, formats) so the copy can be applied in the website build or in Figma; the earlier generated ad-style layouts are kept as `*_ad_*.jpg` for reference.

**Visual identity system (applies to all concepts)**
- Palette: Warm Ivory `#F7F1E7` (dominant), Soft White `#FCFBF8`, Warm Stone `#D9D0C4`, Charcoal `#252521`, Forest Green `#07392C` (accent only), Muted Antique Gold `#B59A65` (jewelry-level accents: thin rules, small caps labels).
- Typography: high-contrast luxury serif headlines; clean modern sans-serif support; heavy hierarchy; readable in 2–3 seconds.
- Photography: cinematic, realistic, editorial; natural window/golden-hour light; limestone, walnut, linen, leather, brass; no orange over-grading; no AI gloss.
- Never: gavels, scales, courthouses, blue-gradient attorney ads, urgency banners, "we fight for you," superlatives, fabricated credentials/testimonials.
- Brand anchor on every asset: the words **REAL TRUST LAW** plus practice-area line "Estate Planning · Wills & Trusts · Real Estate."
- Note: no logo files were supplied with this brief, so the wordmark is typeset in-image. For production, replace the generated wordmark with the real RTL logo lockup in post (Figma/Photoshop) before trafficking any ad.

**Florida Bar advertising posture (all concepts):** factual service descriptions only ("Estate Planning Attorney," "Legal guidance for South Florida families"); no superlatives, rankings, guarantees, implied Bar endorsement, fabricated awards/testimonials/results; people other than Natalie are clearly campaign talent representing clients/family, not lawyers of the firm.
