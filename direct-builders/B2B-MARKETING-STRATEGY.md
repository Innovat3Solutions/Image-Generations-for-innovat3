# Direct Builders — B2B Marketing Strategy

**Objective:** Establish Direct Builders as the go-to "contractor on hand" for HOAs / community associations, property management companies, and developers across Miami-Dade & Broward — converting a 25-year residential + commercial track record into recurring, relationship-based B2B revenue.

**Prepared:** August 2026 · Based on the brand identity, copy, and portfolio at [directbuilders.net](https://www.directbuilders.net/)

B2B buyers — a CAM managing 14 associations, a developer with a 40-unit pipeline — buy something different: **capacity, responsiveness, compliance, and accountability across many projects**. The strategy below repositions without abandoning the residential base.

## 2. Target Audiences (ICPs)

### ICP 1 — HOAs & Condo Associations (via boards and their CAMs)
- **Who:** Board presidents/treasurers and, more importantly, the **licensed Community Association Managers (CAMs)** and management firms who advise them. In practice, the CAM writes the vendor shortlist.
- **Their world right now:** Florida's post-Surfside condo-safety laws (milestone structural inspections at 25/30 years, Structural Integrity Reserve Studies, Miami-Dade/Broward 40-year recertification) have forced associations into **mandatory, funded repair work** — concrete restoration, waterproofing, roofing, railings, life-safety. Budgets exist; qualified, insured GCs who can navigate county permitting are the bottleneck.
- **They buy:** compliance-driven repairs, common-area renovations (lobbies, clubhouses, gyms, pool decks), recurring maintenance, emergency response (storm/water damage).
- **They fear:** unlicensed/underinsured vendors, liens, projects that stall mid-permit, contractors who won't show up for a $15k job after finishing the $500k one.

### ICP 2 — Property Management Companies
- **Who:** Directors of maintenance / regional PMs at residential (multifamily + single-family portfolio) and commercial property management firms in Miami-Dade & Broward.
- **They buy:** unit turns and make-readies, tenant improvement buildouts for commercial landlords, CapEx projects (roofs, facades, parking), insurance-claim reconstruction, 24/7 emergency response.
- **They value:** one accountable vendor across trades, predictable pricing (unit-turn menus, TI cost-per-SF ranges), fast written estimates, W-9/COI paperwork that's always current, and a PM-style communication cadence (weekly written updates — which Direct Builders already promises).

### ICP 3 — Developers & Owner/Investors
- **Who:** Small-to-mid South Florida developers, GC-shopping owner-builders, franchisees and multi-site operators (retail/medical groups opening locations), and tenant-rep/commercial brokers who steer buildout work.
- **They buy:** ground-up commercial, shell-to-suite buildouts, value-add repositioning of aging retail/office, residential spec builds.
- **They value:** pre-construction input (budgeting from schematic drawings), schedule reliability, and a GC who protects their pro forma. Brokers value a GC who makes *them* look good to their client — treat brokers as a referral channel, not just a gatekeeper.

**Priority order:** Start with **ICP 2 (property managers)** — shortest sales cycle, recurring volume, and each PM firm is a multiplier (one relationship = dozens of properties). ICP 1 rides on the same CAM relationships. ICP 3 is the long game, fed by the case-study engine.

## 4. Offer Architecture — productize "contractor on hand"

B2B buyers don't want to "get a quote"; they want a *program*. Package the relationship:

1. **Preferred Vendor Program (for PM firms & CAMs)** — free to join; Direct Builders provides: pre-negotiated rate card, 24–48h written estimates, guaranteed emergency response window, dedicated account PM, quarterly portfolio walk-throughs, always-current COI/W-9 packet. *This is the flagship B2B offer — the thing outreach invites them into.*
2. **Association Repair & Recertification Package (for HOAs/condos)** — engineer's-report-to-completion service: scope translation, board presentation support, permitting, phased construction with resident communication plan, closeout docs for the county.
3. **Tenant Improvement Program (for commercial landlords/brokers)** — standardized TI process with cost-per-SF budget ranges by finish level, so landlords can quote spaces faster.
4. **Pre-Construction Partnership (for developers)** — complimentary budget review on schematic drawings for qualified projects; converts into negotiated GC contracts.

Each program gets a one-page PDF (see Section 6) and a landing page.

## 5. Channel Plan

### A. Relationship & association marketing (highest ROI for this buyer — lead with it)
- **CAI (Community Associations Institute) — SE Florida chapter:** join as a business partner; sponsor CAM education events; exhibit at the annual trade show. This is where CAMs literally go to find vendors.
- **BOMA Miami-Dade, IREM South Florida chapters:** for commercial PM relationships.
- **Latin Builders Association & local GC/developer ecosystem:** developer deal flow + subcontractor network credibility.
- **Lunch-and-learns:** 30-minute sessions at PM company offices — "Navigating milestone inspection repairs in Miami-Dade" / "What your board needs in a repair proposal." Educational, not salesy; Mike Amador presents, leave-behind is the program one-pager. Target: 2/month.
- **Broker/architect referral loop:** quarterly breakfast for tenant-rep brokers and the architects Direct Builders already works with; make referring easy (co-branded capability sheet).

### B. Direct outreach (the engine)
- Build a named-account list: ~150 PM/CAM firms and ~50 active developers in Miami-Dade & Broward (sources: CAI directory, DBPR CAM license rolls, county permit filings, LinkedIn).
- **Cadence per account (4 weeks):** LinkedIn connect from Mike/Frank → personalized email #1 (relevant case study) → call → email #2 (Preferred Vendor one-pager) → physical mailer for top-25 accounts (printed case-study booklet — construction is a tactile industry, print stands out).
- Email templates live in `/direct-builders/assets-checklist.md` scope; keep them 90% about *their* portfolio, 10% about Direct Builders.

### C. LinkedIn (primary digital channel for all three ICPs)
- Create/upgrade the **company page** and make **Mike Amador's profile** the spearhead (founder-led content outperforms company posts ~5:1 in construction).
- Cadence: 3 posts/week — (1) project progress/drone footage with a lesson, (2) case study or client video clip, (3) practical compliance/permitting insight for boards & PMs.
- Team visibility: Frank Alvarez (Executive PM) and Raiza Bengochea (Permitting) posting occasionally makes the "in-house team" pillar tangible.
- Paid: retarget website visitors + sponsored posts to job titles (Community Association Manager, Property Manager, Director of Facilities, Development Manager) in Miami/Fort Lauderdale metro. Modest budget ($1.5–2.5k/mo) — this is air cover for outreach, not the main engine.

### D. Website & SEO (convert the B2B visitor the outreach creates)
- Add a **"For Property Managers & Associations"** section with three landing pages: `/property-management-contractor-miami/`, `/hoa-condo-association-contractor-miami/`, `/developer-general-contractor-miami/` — mirroring the existing service-page URL pattern.
- Each page: program description, named case studies, video testimonial embed, compliance credentials (license #, insurance limits, bonding capacity), and a **B2B-specific form** ("Portfolio size / number of properties" instead of "Estimated budget").
- Add "Request our Capability Statement (PDF)" as a soft-conversion CTA for early-stage buyers.
- SEO targets: "condo association contractor Miami," "milestone inspection repair contractor," "40 year recertification contractor Miami," "tenant improvement contractor Miami," "unit turn contractor Broward."
- Google Business Profile: add commercial categories + B2B photos; reviews from commercial clients (ask Goldberg & Rosen, Dr. Ross, Jack Hickey — they're already on camera).

### E. Email nurture
- Monthly newsletter to the B2B list: one project spotlight, one permitting/compliance tip, one "available capacity" note (PMs love knowing who has crews free).
- Segmented tracks: HOA/CAM (compliance-heavy) vs. PM (operations-heavy) vs. developer (pre-con/budget-heavy).

### F. Video & YouTube (they already have the channel)
- Repurpose existing video testimonials into 30–60s vertical cuts for LinkedIn.
- New series: **"From First Call to Final Walkthrough"** — one episode per process phase, shot on real job sites; doubles as the sales-deck spine.
- Drone before/after flyovers of every commercial project (already their photographic signature — lean in).

## 7. Sales Process & Infrastructure

- **CRM:** stand up a lightweight CRM (HubSpot free tier or Pipedrive) before outreach begins. Pipeline stages: Target → Contacted → Meeting → Program Enrolled / Bid List → First Project → Repeat Client. Track *accounts* (PM firms, associations) not just projects.
- **Speed standard:** every B2B inquiry gets a human response inside 4 business hours and a written estimate inside 48h for standard scopes — then *say so publicly* ("48-hour written estimates") because almost no Miami GC commits to it.
- **Compliance kit ready-to-send:** COI, W-9, license, safety program, references in one PDF/folder link. PMs disqualify vendors who take a week to produce paperwork.
- **Owner's role:** Mike Amador's calendar gets 4 hrs/week of pure B2B relationship time (lunch-and-learns, CAI events, top-account calls). Frank Alvarez owns program delivery so sales promises land.

## 11. Risks & watch-outs

- **Capacity credibility:** don't enroll more Preferred Vendor accounts than crews can serve — a missed emergency response call undoes a year of marketing. Cap enrollments per quarter.
- **Small-job resentment:** the model only works if $10k jobs are treated like $500k jobs. Staff a dedicated service/small-projects crew as B2B volume grows.
- **Residential brand dilution:** keep residential pages and lead flow intact; B2B lives in its own site section and campaigns.
- **Compliance messaging accuracy:** condo-safety statutes keep evolving — have counsel or the association's engineer validate any compliance claims in published guides.
