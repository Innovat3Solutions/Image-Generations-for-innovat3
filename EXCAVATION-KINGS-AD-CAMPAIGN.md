# Excavation Kings — 10-Ad Social Campaign

Ten unique 4:5 social ads, one visual mechanic each, never repeated. Same brand world as the website concept (asphalt `#141414`, safety yellow `#F2B90D`, bone `#EDEAE3`, condensed caps display type, amber-dust cinematic grade, crown + wordmark, tagline "WE DIG IT BETTER"). No em dashes in any ad copy.

Renders use a placeholder crown + "EXCAVATION KINGS" wordmark; swap in the real logo file at production. Contact placeholders: (704) 555-0119 · excavationkings.com.
