# Talira Marketing Strategy

**Talira** · AI voice agents, built and implemented for small businesses · taliravoice.com

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** Talira builds and installs AI voice agents for small businesses, a 24/7 receptionist that never takes a day off, answers in under half a second, and makes sure no lead ever goes to voicemail.

**The critical distinction:** Talira is not a DIY software tool. It's a done-for-you team. Competitors sell dashboards and leave the owner to figure it out; Talira builds the agent, trains it on the business, wires it into the calendar and CRM, and keeps improving it. Sell the *implementation team*, not the software.

**Positioning statement:**
> For small business owners who lose leads every time the phone rings unanswered, Talira is the team that builds and runs your AI receptionist, so every call gets answered 24/7, every appointment gets booked, and every lead gets captured. Unlike DIY AI tools, we implement everything for you.

### Brand voice
- Direct, confident, a little bold, never corporate-stiff.
- Talks about the owner's money and time, not about "AI innovation."
- Myth-busting energy: happy to challenge assumptions ("AI sounds robotic," "we're too small for this").

## 3. Target audience (ICP)

**Primary:** owner-operators of appointment-driven local businesses, 1–20 employees, where a missed call is directly lost revenue:
- Med/dental/health clinics · Real estate agents · Law firms · Restaurants · Car dealerships · Home services (HVAC, plumbing, roofing) · Salons & med spas · E-commerce support lines

**Their reality:**
- The phone rings while they're with a customer, on a job, or asleep. ~62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered; ~85% of callers who reach voicemail don't call back, they call the next business on the list.
- A full-time receptionist costs $35–45K/yr + benefits, works 40 of the week's 168 hours, takes vacations, and calls in sick.
- They've heard of AI but assume it's robotic, complicated, or "for big companies."

**Buying trigger moments:** hiring a receptionist (or failing to), losing a big lead to voicemail, seasonal call spikes, receptionist quitting, expanding hours.

## 4. Core messages & proof points

| Pain | Message | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Missed calls | "Never miss a call again" | 24/7 answering, <500ms response |
| Cost of staffing | "A receptionist that costs less than a part-timer" | ~80% cost reduction vs. hired support |
| Lost leads | "3x higher lead conversion" | Instant answer + instant follow-up |
| Skepticism | "It doesn't sound like a robot" | Human-like voices, 40+ languages; free 5-min live demo call |
| Complexity | "We build it for you" | Done-for-you setup, calendar + CRM integration, human-transfer fallback |

**Offer ladder:**
1. **Free**, live 5-minute demo call with a Talira agent (the "aha" moment; every ad should drive here).
2. **Growth Partner**, $497/mo + $1,500 setup: dedicated agent, CRM sync, calendar booking, knowledge base, ongoing tuning.

## 5. Content pillars (social)

1. **Myth vs. Fact**, bust AI/receptionist myths. (Ad concept #1)
2. **The Hiring Flip**, job-post-style ads for "a receptionist that works 24/7, no sick days." (Ad concept #2)
3. **The Missed Call**, dramatize the 9:47 PM call that went to voicemail. Loss-framing. (Ad concept #3)
4. **Numbers That Hurt/Help**, one big stat per post (62% unanswered, 85% never call back, 80% cost cut, 3x conversion). (Ad concept #4)
5. **Behind the Build**, the team implementing an agent for a real business type; positions Talira as builders, not a SaaS login.
6. **Industry Spotlights**, "For dental clinics: …" rotating through verticals; same template, swapped pain.

**Cadence:** 4–5 posts/week, 1 myth/fact, 1 hiring-flip or missed-call, 1 stat, 1 industry spotlight, 1 demo push/testimonial. Every post CTA: **"Hear it live, free 5-minute demo call → taliravoice.com"**

## 6. Channels & funnel

- **Instagram/Facebook (primary paid + organic):** the 4:5 creatives in `generations/talira/`. Retarget site visitors with the demo-call offer.
- **LinkedIn (organic):** founder-voice posts using the same pillars, aimed at clinic managers/law firm admins.
- **Google Search (high intent):** "answering service," "virtual receptionist," "after hours answering service [city/vertical]", landing on a vertical-specific page.
- **Cold email/DM to verticals:** hook = their own missed-call math ("10 missed calls a week × $200 avg job = $8K/month on the table").

**Funnel:** Ad → taliravoice.com → free 5-min live demo call → strategy call → Growth Partner ($497/mo). The demo call *is* the product proof, minimize every step between ad and demo.

## 7. Launch calendar (first 30 days)

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Post ad set organically (myth/fact, hiring flip, missed call). Start $10–20/day boosts on the hiring flip + missed call. |
| 2 | Add stat post + industry spotlight #1 (highest-value vertical first, e.g., dental/med spa). Launch Google Search on "virtual receptionist" terms. |
| 3 | Retargeting audience live; push demo-call ad to site visitors. First cold-email batch to one vertical. |
| 4 | Double budget on the winning creative, kill the losers, produce v2 of winner. Publish first case study / demo-call recording clip. |

**KPIs:** cost per demo call (target < $30 at start), demo → strategy call rate, CPL by creative, vertical with cheapest CPL (that becomes the niche to own).
