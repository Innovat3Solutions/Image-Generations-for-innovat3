# Talira V2 Ad Set: Captions + Model Comparison

Ten new concepts, each on purpose visually distinct from the first six and from each other. Four image models were tested head to head. No em dashes anywhere, in the creatives or in this copy.

## Model comparison results

| Model | Cost/img | Ads | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano Banana 2 (Google, via fal.ai) | $0.08 | v2-01, v2-02 | Best all-rounder. Perfect logo reproduction, clean text, reliable layouts. Keep as the default. |
| Nano Banana Pro (Google, via fal.ai) | $0.15 | v2-03, v2-04 | Best overall quality of the batch. The split screen and comparison table are the two most complex layouts and both came out flawless. Use for finals and complex compositions. |
| Seedream 5.0 Pro (ByteDance, via fal.ai) | $0.0675 | v2-05, v2-06 | Gorgeous photography (the door note shot is the most cinematic of all 16). Weaknesses: logo mark gets slightly warped, and dense UI text falls apart (calendar weekday labels garbled). Use for photo-led concepts with little UI text. |
| FLUX.2 Flex (Black Forest Labs, via fal.ai) | ~$0.13 | v2-07, v2-08 | Strong product-photography look and solid headline text. Logo soundwave slightly deformed on both. Good second opinion for photographic scenes. |
| Ideogram V4 (via fal.ai) | ~$0.06 | v2-09, v2-10 | Best pure typography and cheapest. Cannot take the logo file (text-to-image only), so the wordmark is typeset text and the real logo must be added in post. Great for type-only stat posters. |

**Recommendation:** iterate drafts on Nano Banana 2, produce finals on Nano Banana Pro at 2K, use Seedream/FLUX for photo-led one-offs, Ideogram for typographic stat posters (drop the real logo on afterward).

**Spend for this batch:** 10 images ≈ $0.98 actual-estimate across the four providers' meters.

Known flaws to fix before paid use: v2-06 calendar weekday labels are garbled (re-roll on Nano Banana Pro), v2-05/07/08 logo mark slightly warped (paste the real PNG over it in post, or re-roll on a Nano Banana model).
